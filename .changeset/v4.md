entrepta 4.0: the library without the mark. The ◆ and the `//` stop being defaults, the editor look leaves the registry, and a seventh theme, taylor, is flat and monochrome. Each breaking change says how to bring the old look back: https://entrepta.vercel.app/docs/migrating-to-v4

**Breaking**

- Labels (CardLabel, FieldLabel, DialogLabel, Metric, ChoiceCard, SwatchPicker, ChatThread) draw nothing in front of their text. Pass `icon={<Diamond />}` for the old ◆.
- Sidebar, MobileNav and Stepper mark the current item with an `indicator`, a dot by default. Tabs and TabNav take one too and have none by default. `null` hides it.
- FieldError, the command palette footer and the menu titles lose the `//` and the ◆. CardComment is now CardNote, the text alone.
- `lib/format.ts` is `lib/entrepta-format.ts`, since the old name collided with files projects already have. Reinstall what imports it.
- Removed from the registry: StatusBar and ChromeMessage (they live in the docs app now, and ChromeMessage's job is EmptyState with `size="page"`), SectHead, doc-parts, PageOutline, the Card `terminal` variant and the Tabs `window` variant. `add` names each one and links the guide.

**New**

- taylor, a theme with no hue: white on dark, black on light, no brand glow. `--finish-glow` (0 to 1) sets how much brand light a card and an overlay carry, and `--chart-from` sets where the chart colors start turning.
- EmptyState takes an `illustration`, `size="page"` for a 404 or an error screen, and a `headingLevel`.
- ChatThread takes an avatar for each role. Tooltip takes `surface="page"`. SegmentedControl takes `shape="pill"` and `truncate={false}`. Diamond has its own page.
- The star of a required label is drawn by CSS, so a test finds the label by its name. The sidebar rail's tooltip clears the rail's border.
