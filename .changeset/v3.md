entrepta 3.0: the components a product needs, on top of everything 2.x has. Nothing in 2.x breaks.

- A chart palette with no fixed color. `--chart-1` to `--chart-8` are the brand's hue turned in steps of 45°, at one lightness per mode that clears 3:1 on a card, so every theme recolors its charts by itself. Run `init --overwrite` to get the tokens.
- `useUrlFilterList`, next to `useUrlFilter`, for a filter with several values (`?category=a&category=b`). Values outside the allowed list are dropped.
- `useCopy`, the copy logic CodeBlock uses, as its own hook: it reports `copied` or `error` and never claims a copy that did not happen.
- **Popover**: a panel anchored to a trigger, for content you work in, on the overlay surface.
- **Sheet**: a panel from the edge to create or edit without leaving the list. From the right on a desktop and from the bottom below 640px. With `dirty`, closing asks first, inside the sheet. Dialog exports its close button as `DialogCloseButton`.
- Resizing a Textarea follows the cursor. It eased every property over 150ms, its height included, so the box trailed behind the handle. Input and Button name the properties they ease too.
- **Amount**: money shown one way everywhere, from integer minor units. Mono with tabular figures, the real minus sign, the symbol in the muted ink. `tone="auto"` colors by the sign and always shows it. `compact` keeps the full value for screen readers and hover.
- **MoneyInput**: money typed like a cash machine (digits from the right, caret pinned to the end) or freely. Any pasted format works (`R$ 1.234,56`, `€1,234.56`, `1234.5`), and the value is an integer, never a float.
- **FormatProvider** (`use-format`) sets the locale, currency and time zone once, and **`lib/format`** holds the formatting and parsing they use: money in minor units from `Intl` (JPY has none, BHD three), plain `YYYY-MM-DD` dates that no time zone can shift.
- Input exports its frame and field classes (`inputWrapperVariants`, `inputFieldClass`) for controls that share its look.
- **Select**: a value from a short list, with the field's look and the menu's rows. It takes a Field's label and error.
- **Combobox**: a value from a long list you can search. Groups, several values, create on the spot, a suggested option to confirm. The search ranks a run of the letters typed, the start of a name first, and ignores accents, instead of matching letters scattered across words.
- **SegmentedControl**: one choice out of a few, on native radios, with an indicator that slides in CSS.
- **Calendar**, **DatePicker** and **DateNavigator**: a day, a range with presets, a month or a year, as plain `YYYY-MM-DD` strings in and out. Today is today in the account's time zone; every word and the first day of the week come from the locale. Built on react-day-picker 10 with every piece replaced.
- CommandInput takes `size="sm"`, for a search inside a popover.
